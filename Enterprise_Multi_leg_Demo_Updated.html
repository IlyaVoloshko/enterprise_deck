Summary

  • Multi-leg scheduling logic supports different combinations of Scheduled and Will Call legs.
  • Scheduling Engine automatically calculates Pickup Times based on Appointment Time, Travel Time, and the sequence of all trip legs.
  • The same scheduling logic is consistently applied to both Trips and Standing Orders.
  • All demonstrated scenarios are fully supported for Trip creation and Standing Order creation.
